# Music, from score to sound

ScoreGlide develops an AI music expert and models for reading scores, following performances, and understanding music.

Our score-following videos bring the written notes and the recording together. Musical explanations connect what the score says with what can be heard in the performance.

**[Explore the demo](https://demo.scoreglide.com/?lang=en)** · [Read in Chinese](README.zh-CN.md) · [Contact us](mailto:hello@scoreglide.com)

The public demo features selected video previews and prepared musical analysis. The full app is currently available by invitation.
