# ScoreGlide

**See the score move with the performance.**

ScoreGlide aligns a musical score with a performance recording to create videos where visual markers follow the notes. We also explore musical explanations and practice suggestions grounded in the score and recording.

[Watch the demo](https://demo.scoreglide.com/?lang=en) · [Read in Chinese](README.zh-CN.md) · [Contact us](mailto:hello@scoreglide.com)

## What we're building

- **Score-following videos:** Watch the written music and hear the performance together.
- **Musical insight:** Explore a piece's structure, performance details, and ideas for practice.
- **Careful analysis:** Make recognition and alignment uncertainty visible rather than presenting technical guesses as performance mistakes.

## Current status

ScoreGlide is in private beta. The public demo features selected video previews and prepared examples of musical analysis.
